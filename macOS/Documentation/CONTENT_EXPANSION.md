# Content Expansion

The season is expanded from four episodes to eight.

## New episodes

### Episode 5 — Laundry Room Lockup
Focus: laundry products, cleaners, original containers, and not mixing household cleaners.

### Episode 6 — Garden Shed Panic
Focus: yard chemicals, pesticides, fuels, labeling, and locked storage.

### Episode 7 — Someone Else's House
Focus: unfamiliar pills and household products while visiting another home.

### Episode 8 — Poison Control Challenge
A mixed-hazard final safety test using all four hazard/enemy families and the densest level layout in the game.

## New visual themes

Four new procedural SpriteKit backdrops were added for laundry, garden/shed, guest-house, and final-challenge stages. No new external dependencies are required.
